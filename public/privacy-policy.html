Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gladiators Painting Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use AI Front Desk Helper (aifrontdeskhelper.com) and our related services, including our website, web chat widget, SMS messaging, voice calling, and dashboard application (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, mailing address, and phone number when you sign up, request a quote, or contact us
  • Business information when you onboard as a customer (company name, service area, business hours)
  • Payment information processed by our payment provider (Stripe) — we do not store full card numbers
  • Content of messages, calls, and chats you send through the Services

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used, time spent)
  • Cookies and similar technologies for session management and analytics

Information from third parties

  • Communication metadata from Twilio when you call or text a number associated with our Services
  • Lead data from Facebook Messenger when you contact us via Facebook
  • Calendar events from Google Calendar when integrated by our customers

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Services
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule appointments, and follow up on leads on behalf of our business customers
  • To send transactional and service-related communications (booking confirmations, follow-ups, account notifications)
  • To send SMS messages you have opted in to receive
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations
SMS / Text Message Privacy

We do not sell, rent, share, transfer, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information collected for SMS communication is used solely to provide the Services you have requested. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for assistance.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as necessary to provide the Services or as required by law:

  • Service providers: Third parties that help us operate the Services (Twilio for SMS/voice, OpenAI for AI conversation, Resend for email, Stripe for payments, Supabase for database hosting, Render and AWS for infrastructure). These providers process information only on our behalf and are bound by contract to protect your data.
  • Our business customers: If you contact a business that uses AI Front Desk Helper (for example, by calling or texting their number), the conversation content and lead information you provide is shared with that business so they can serve you.
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

We do not sell, rent, share, transfer, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

4. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: You may request access to or correction of your personal information by emailing us.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • SMS opt-out: Reply STOP to any SMS message, or email us, to stop SMS communications at any time.
  • Email opt-out: Click the unsubscribe link in any marketing email to stop email communications.
  • Cookies: You can control cookies through your browser settings.
  • California residents: Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have additional rights including the right to know, delete, and opt out of sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • EU/UK residents: Under GDPR/UK GDPR, you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Services and comply with legal obligations. Conversation transcripts and call recordings are retained for up to 24 months unless a longer period is required for fraud prevention, legal hold, or compliance. Account data is retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure to handle billing and legal matters.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

The Services are operated in the United States. If you access the Services from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S., where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email (if we have your address) or by prominent notice on our website. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
