# Onboarding Guide: Roofe CRM + AI Front Desk Integration

This guide details the steps to connect a new location using **Roofe CRM** to the AI Front Desk system. 

## Integration Overview
The integration uses two Zapier webhooks to sync data between the systems.

### Zap 1: From Roofe to AI Front Desk (Lead Capture)
This Zap sends estimate and contact data to AI Front Desk to initiate the sales recovery and booking process.

1.  **Trigger:** Roofe CRM "New Estimate Sent" (or similar webhook).
2.  **Action:** Webhook "POST" to AI Front Desk.
    - **URL:** `https://your-api-domain.com/api/webhooks/crm/estimate-sent`
    - **Payload Type:** JSON
    - **Data Mapping Required:**
        - `api_key`: (Found in AI Front Desk Settings)
        - `contact_phone`: (Lead's phone number)
        - `contact_name`: (Lead's full name)
        - `estimated_revenue_cents`: (Total amount in CENTS. e.g., $1000.00 = 100000)
        - `crm_type`: `roofe`

### Zap 2: From AI Front Desk to Roofe (Booking Sync)
This Zap pulls booking details and call summaries back into Roofe CRM when a call is handled or an appointment is made.

1.  **Trigger:** "Catch Hook" by Zapier.
    - Use the URL generated by Zapier and paste it into AI Front Desk **Settings > Zapier Webhook URL**.
2.  **Action:** Create or Update Lead/Service in Roofe.
    - **Mapping:**
        - Map `name`, `phone`, and `email` to Roofe contact fields.
        - Map `summary` or `transcript` to a Note or History field in Roofe.

### Troubleshooting & Common Issues

#### 1. Revenue is showing up as $0.xx or very low
AI Front Desk expects `estimated_revenue_cents` to be an **integer in cents**.
- **Correct:** $1,500.00 → `150000`
- **Incorrect:** $1,500.00 → `1500.00`
- **Fix:** In your Zapier Formatter step, multiply the Roofe "Estimate Total" by 100 before sending it to AI Front Desk.

#### 2. Zap 1 Trigger not firing
Ensure that the "Estimate Sent" event in Roofe is actually triggering the Catch Hook. 
- Check the "Zapier Task History" to see if the payload is reaching Zapier.
- Ensure the `api_key` in the JSON body exactly matches the key found in AI Front Desk **Settings > API Key**.

#### 3. Data not pulling into Roofe (Zap 2)
The "Zapier Webhook URL" in AI Front Desk **Settings** must be a "Catch Hook" URL from Zapier.
- If this URL is empty, AI Front Desk will not push data out.
- Test by making a test booking on your AI number and checking the Zapier "Test Trigger" section.
